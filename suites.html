What Is a Suite?

Private Salon Suites, Built for Independence

A salon suite gives independent beauty professionals the opportunity to work from a private, professional environment while creating an experience that reflects their own brand. Rather than sharing a floor or a single chair, you operate within a dedicated space that is entirely yours to run as a business owner.

  • A private, professional setting for your services
  • Freedom to shape the client experience around your brand
  • Operate as the owner of your own beauty business
